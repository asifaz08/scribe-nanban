# Scribe Nanban – உங்கள் தேர்வு நண்பன்

### An Accessible Digital Examination Assistance Platform

**Tagline: Your Voice. Your Answer. Your Independence.**

---

## 1. About the Project

Scribe Nanban is a web-based examination assistance platform designed to support students with motor disabilities who experience difficulties with conventional writing and typing.

The platform explores alternative interaction methods such as voice input, text-to-speech and accessible navigation to make examinations more inclusive.

Our goal is to reduce physical interaction barriers and provide students with a more accessible examination experience.

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional examination systems primarily depend on handwriting or keyboard-based input.

Students with motor disabilities or limited hand mobility may face difficulties:

- Writing examination answers independently.
- Typing for extended periods.
- Navigating conventional examination interfaces.
- Communicating their knowledge through traditional methods.

**The core problem:** Physical limitations should not prevent students from demonstrating their academic knowledge.

## 3. Proposed Solution

Scribe Nanban aims to provide an accessible digital examination platform that supports alternative methods of interaction.

It focuses on voice-based responses, text-to-speech, simplified navigation and customizable accessibility settings.

The platform aims to make examinations more accessible, comfortable and inclusive.

## 4. Key Features

- **Bilingual Support:** English and Tamil interface.
- **Voice Interaction:** Speech recognition and text-to-speech.
- **Switch Navigation:** Alternative navigation using keyboard controls.
- **Customizable Interface:** Light, dark and calm themes.
- **Text Adjustment:** Adjustable text size.
- **Examination Timer:** Timer with additional-time settings.
- **Answer Management:** Review, edit and delete responses.
- **Local Storage:** Retains selected information in the browser.
- **Breathing Break:** Optional short break feature.
- **Results Interface:** Answer review and copy functionality.

## 6. How It Works

1. Student opens the Scribe Nanban website.
2. Selects language and accessibility preferences.
3. Enters the examination interface.
4. Views examination questions.
5. Provides answers using available input methods.
6. Reviews and edits answers.
7. Completes the examination and views the results interface.

## 8. Current Development Status

**Project Stage: Frontend Prototype**

The current version is developed using HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Implemented features include the website interface, bilingual support, voice interaction, switch navigation, themes, examination timer, answer management and local storage.

Backend integration, cloud database, AI-powered assistance and live student-scribe matching are planned for future development.
